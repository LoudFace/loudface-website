02 · The evidence

What ChatGPT actually said

Prompt: "What is LoudFace? Tell me about the agency — what they do, who they work with, and what makes them different. Cite your sources." (gpt-5.5, web search on)

The differentiator — straight from the page

"Founder-led operating model — their own brand information describes the agency as a founder-led studio with full-time specialists rather than a generic retainer shop." cited: loudface.co/ai-instructions — this phrasing exists ONLY on the new page

That's the canary. The "lifestyle business / cookie-cutter retainer" contrast we planted came back as "generic retainer shop," attributed to the page. There's nowhere else on the site it could have come from — attribution confirmed.

The page was a top source for the core facts

/ai-instructions was cited as the source for: who LoudFace serves (Series A+, <$100M ARR), HQ (Dubai, fully remote), the "one team builds + grows" model, and the AEO positioning. 6 citations in one answer

On the superlative — the guardrail held

"I can't verify that LoudFace is the fastest-growing agency… I did not find an independent ranking, revenue/headcount growth data, or third-party benchmark proving 'fastest-growing.'" ChatGPT, second prompt — then it repeated the reason-why framing instead

This is why we framed the claim as positioning + reason rather than raw fact. A bare "we're #1" would've been ignored or hedged; the reason ("founder-led, full-time specialists, not cookie-cutter") got picked up cleanly.

03 · What this proves

The mechanism is real

  • It's retrieval, not obedience. ChatGPT didn't "follow instructions" — it found the cleanest, most quotable brand-fact source and lifted from it. Exactly the prediction in the briefing.
  • Speed matches the original. Nectiv said ~48h; we hit it at ~48h. The IndexNow + footer-link + fact-dense structure did its job.
  • The canary did its job. A unique phrase let us attribute a citation with certainty, not vibes.
  • Honest framing beat hype. The defensible reason-why traveled; the superlative didn't. Worth remembering for client playbooks.
04 · Honest caveats

What this is NOT (yet)

Sample sizeOne ChatGPT-search call (via DataForSEO's ChatGPT scraper — represents the real search mechanism, but it's a single sample). Re-run on a few prompt variants to confirm it's stable, not a one-off.
Peec won't catch itPeec tracks category prompts ("best AEO agency for B2B SaaS"), not branded "what is LoudFace" ones. So this win won't show in Peec dashboards. To track it, we'd add a branded prompt to Peec or keep spot-checking live ChatGPT.
ChatGPT-only so farHaven't tested Gemini / Claude / Google AI. Nectiv saw ChatGPT-only. Expect the same unless tested otherwise.
Not in Google indexThe page isn't in Google's classic index yet (48h is early). Doesn't matter for the ChatGPT result — different pipeline — but worth submitting in Search Console to speed it.
