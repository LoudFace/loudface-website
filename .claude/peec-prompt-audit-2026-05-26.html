What to remove

These are tracked but track the wrong thing. Either remove from Peec or move to a "content research" view that's not on the visibility KPI list.

  • "How long does AEO take to show results for a B2B SaaS company" remove Educational, 0% cited, Comp 23. Marketer-research query, not buyer-decision. Doesn't drive pipeline.
  • "How to build organic traffic for a B2B SaaS website" remove Thought leadership, 0% cited. Blog-discovery query. The reader is doing research, not buying.
  • "How to track AI search visibility for a SaaS company" remove Educational. Same problem.
  • "Looking for a performance marketing agency using Webflow" remove Wrong category — LoudFace is not a performance marketing agency. The buyer here isn't a fit.
  • "Webflow experts for improving organic traffic through AEO" remove Webflow-front-door framing contradicts the May 2026 repositioning where Webflow is delivery capability, not product.
  • "How to increase demo request conversions on a SaaS website" remove Top-of-funnel marketing research. Comp 124, LF 0. Even winning it wouldn't drive deals.
  • "Best organic growth strategies for B2B SaaS in 2026" remove Educational. Reader research, not buyer query.

What to add — the 5 critical gaps

These are missing entirely. Each maps to a specific commercial reality (case study, ICP, pricing tier, or pipeline signal) that the current tracker doesn't measure.

  • "LoudFace agency reviews" + "LoudFace vs Radyant" + "LoudFace vs Omnius" + "LoudFace vs SaaSHero" add (4-5 prompts) Zero branded prompts tracked today. Branded queries are where bottom-funnel demand surfaces — without these we can't measure when content actually shifts brand awareness. Bake-off queries ("LoudFace vs X") are the moment a consideration-stage buyer narrows their shortlist.
  • "Best AEO agency for B2B fintech payroll and payments companies" + "SEO agency for stablecoin / web3 fintech B2B" + "Marketing agency for embedded finance startups" add (5-7 prompts) All 7 fintech prompts currently tracked are generic. Zero capture LoudFace's actual fintech wedge (Toku stablecoin payroll). The case study with the most evidence has zero prompt support in the tracker.
  • "Series B SaaS organic growth agency" + "Agency for $5M-$20M ARR SaaS scaling organic" + "Growth agency for Series C SaaS pre-IPO" add (3-5 prompts) Only one stage-of-buyer prompt exists today, and it's not cited. Series A-C is half the ICP. These are pipeline-grade queries — the buyer is in budget range.
  • "Agency that combines SEO, AEO, and Webflow for B2B SaaS" + "Full-stack organic growth retainer for SaaS" add (2-3 prompts) The unified-stack positioning (Solo/Dual/Scale) has no prompt that maps to it. This is the differentiation; nothing in the tracker measures it.
  • "How to evaluate a B2B SaaS SEO agency" + "Questions to ask an AEO agency before hiring" + "Red flags when hiring a SaaS growth agency" add (3 prompts) Vendor-evaluation queries are pre-shortlist intent. The agency that publishes the evaluation framework usually gets cited — and we just shipped 9 evaluation-criteria tables today. Perfect alignment between content and prompts.

Structural changes — fix the taxonomy

Clean up the 23 "untracked match" rows

31% of the DB has no Matched LoudFace URLs assigned. They're noise in every dashboard. For each: either assign a target URL (so it becomes a real coverage measurement) or remove. As-is they distort all the rate calculations.

Add a Vertical field to the Topic taxonomy

Current Topic mixes service (AEO, SEO, Webflow) with vertical (B2B SaaS) and intent stage (Converting AI traffic). Split: keep "Topic = service cluster," add a new "Vertical = SaaS / Fintech / B2B-other" multi-select. Only way to filter "how am I doing on fintech?" in one view.

Rebalance the intent mix to commercial 75-80%

Current: 69% commercial / 17% educational. Target: 75-80% commercial, 8-10% pricing, 8-10% comparison/branded, ≤5% educational. Educational is doing too much work for a tracker meant to measure pipeline-relevant visibility.

Audit the AEO topic urgently

44 prompts (29% of total) at 7% citation rate is the biggest single misalignment. Run /pattern-audit "AEO AI Search for SaaS" next session — that's the LoudFace internal command that pulls every URL targeting these prompts and benchmarks against the saved competitor URLs. Either the content's structurally wrong or the prompts are. Both are real possibilities.

Drop "Webflow AEO Agency" topic to 0-3 prompts

14 prompts at 0% citation. The "Webflow agency that does AEO" framing is the legacy positioning the May 2026 brain update specifically killed (Webflow = delivery capability, not front door). Tracking 14 prompts for an explicitly-deprecated framing wastes Peec quota.

The bottom line

The tracker isn't measuring what matters. It's heavily weighted toward Educational + generic Webflow + AEO-research prompts that don't drive pipeline. It's missing the three clusters most aligned with LoudFace's actual proof: fintech-specific commercial, stage-of-buyer (Series A-C), and branded / vs-competitor.

The 9 covered-but-losing commercial prompts are the right targets to win, not replace. Radyant, SaaSHero, DiscoveredLabs, ExaltGrowth, Onely are eating the listicle slot on B2B SaaS SEO. That's a content+citations gap, not a positioning gap.

The 13 Educational and 8 Thought Leadership prompts are the right ones to remove. They're 21 of 75 slots being spent measuring marketer research instead of buyer demand.

Recommended first action: open the Prompt Coverage DB in Notion, archive the 7 specific prompts listed in "What to remove" above, and add the 17–22 prompts listed in "What to add." That alone shifts the corpus from 69% commercial to ~80% commercial and starts measuring the fintech wedge and brand demand that the current tracker is blind to.
