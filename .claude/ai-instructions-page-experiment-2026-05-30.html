Why the canary is clever

Putting a unique fact only on that page turns a vague claim into proof. When ChatGPT repeated "$30M+ ARR," there was only one place it could have come from. That's a real attribution test — and it's the part we should keep.

02 · The trap

This is not llms.txt — and that distinction is everything

The internet is currently conflating two different things. They live at different URLs, get treated differently by crawlers, and have opposite track records.

The hard data on llms.txt

A ~300,000-domain SE Ranking study found no relationship between having llms.txt and how often a domain is cited by LLMs — removing it even improved model accuracy. Separately, monitoring of 500M+ AI-bot visits found only ~408 hits targeting llms.txt directly. If we ship the root file and call it done, we ship the thing that doesn't work.

So the takeaway isn't "make an llms.txt." It's: make a clean, fact-dense, indexable page that becomes the single canonical source of brand facts — and let normal AI search retrieval do the rest.

03 · Mechanism

Why it actually works (it's retrieval, not obedience)

The "instructions for AI" framing is half theatre. ChatGPT isn't taking orders from your page. What's happening is more mundane — and more reliable:

The page wins because it's the densest, cleanest, most quotable source of brand facts — not because it "instructs" anything.

Two real reasons it punches above its weight:

  • It's the canonical answer source. Most sites scatter brand facts across home / about / pricing. One tidy page concentrates them, so it's the easiest thing for a retrieval system to lift a clean answer from.
  • Structure gets cited more. Direct-answer formatting, tables and clear headings get pulled into AI answers far more than prose. The markdown style isn't magic — it's just retrieval-friendly.
04 · The honest limits

What it does not do

ChatGPT only

Nectiv saw it on ChatGPT and nowhere else — Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Mode didn't cite the page on the same prompts. Single-engine play.

Branded queries only

It grounds "who is LoudFace / what do they do" answers. It will not rank you for "best B2B SaaS SEO agency" — that's still the listicle/comparison game.

Not obedience

You can't force a tone or suppress a competitor by writing it down. The model paraphrases facts; it doesn't follow commands in a public file.

Not durable yet

Anecdotal, recent, single-engine. Treat the "48 hours" as one data point, not a law. Hence: measure ours.

One soft risk worth naming

A public "INSTRUCTIONS FOR AI" block can read as manipulative and, in theory, invites prompt-injection-style scrutiny. We'll keep ours as honest, verifiable brand facts framed as an AI-readable fact sheet — not as commands to lie or bury rivals.
