Why their generic article is winning a fintech-specific prompt

Magna's URL is /blog/ai-search-strategies/best-aeo-agency-2026.html — a generic AEO listicle. It doesn't say "fintech" anywhere meaningful. So why does it surface for "20 best AEO agencies for fintech in 2026"?

The substitution mechanic

When an AI model gets a vertical-specific prompt and no article is a perfect match, it falls back to the strongest generic match in the category. Magna optimized hard for the generic AEO prompt with extraction-friendly structure + concentrated entity authority. Our fintech-specific article only shipped today and has zero accumulated authority signals (citations, links, mentions).

The result: AI substitutes their generic top-pick into the fintech query. We're not losing the fintech battle — we haven't shown up to it yet.

This is also why their 2-month head-start matters more than our 6-year SEO history: their entity profile is concentrated 100% on "AEO." Our entity profile splits across Webflow, B2B SaaS, AEO, design, fintech. AI's "who's the AEO agency" answer pulls strongly toward the concentrated signal.

Things we can't see — and shouldn't assume

  • Backlink velocity. A 2-month-old domain hitting top-5 AEO citation may be running aggressive PR/digital-PR campaigns to seed AI training. Worth pulling Ahrefs on usemagna.com to see — separate task.
  • Trustpilot reviews. 4.8/5 across 41 reviews is real-looking, but 41 reviews accumulated in 2 months is fast. Could be legitimate or coordinated.
  • AI Visibility Score widget. Free tool that drives email captures + brand mentions. Tool-led content marketing is one of the strongest AEO plays — AI models cite "tools you can use" sections.
  • Founder/team byline strategy. "August Tange" gets a personal byline. If their leadership is publishing on LinkedIn + bylined posts on third-party publications, that's concentrated AEO entity work.
