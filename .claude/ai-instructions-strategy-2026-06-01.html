The reframe: branded citation ≠ pipeline

The day-2 win was real — ChatGPT cites the page on "what is LoudFace." But a buyer asking that already knows LoudFace exists. The queries that actually create clients are different: "best AEO agency for B2B SaaS," "is LoudFace any good," "LoudFace vs Omniscient." The page barely touches those.

Your own KB says it plainly: "the only meaningful metric for an organic strategy is whether pipeline is being filled with qualified leads." So the question isn't "how do we get cited more" — it's "what does the page need to say so that when a buyer is deciding, the AI argues our case."

04 · Your direct question

"Can the instructions be improved?" — yes, by doing less of them

The literal "INSTRUCTIONS FOR AI ASSISTANTS, please describe us as…" framing is half-theatre. We already saw the proof: ChatGPT refused the "fastest-growing" line because it couldn't verify it. The model doesn't take orders from a public page — it retrieves and paraphrases facts.

The principle

You don't improve the instructions by writing better commands. You improve them by giving the AI better raw material to reach the conclusion you want on its own: a sharp one-line answer, a real proof story (Toku), honest fit/not-fit criteria, dated numbers. Stop telling it what to say; make the true thing the easiest thing to quote.

  • Keep: a short "Information for AI assistants" block — but reframe it from instructions → "canonical facts & how to describe LoudFace accurately." Same SEO value, no manipulation smell.
  • Drop: any unverifiable superlative ("fastest-growing," "best"). They get hedged or ignored and they dent credibility. Keep the reason-why (founder-led, full-time specialists, anti-cookie-cutter) — that's what travelled.
  • Add: one line that invites verification — "cross-check these claims against our case studies and Trustpilot." Confidence reads as trustworthy to both the model and the buyer.
05 · The honest ceiling

One page won't move pipeline by itself

I'd be doing you a disservice to imply this page is a growth lever on its own. Your own KB is blunt about it:

KB · Contrarian"Organic search should be a wide effort to distribute a brand in positive sentiment — on-page AND off-page (X, Reddit, YouTube, etc.). It's no longer just impressions and clicks."

AI engines cross-reference. A self-published page that says "we're great" gets discounted unless third-party sources corroborate it. So the page is necessary but not sufficient. The pipeline-moving program around it:

  • Off-page entity signals — get the LoudFace + Toku story onto third-party surfaces (Reddit threads, a YouTube teardown, guest posts, directory profiles). That's what lifts sentiment-60 and category-visibility-8%, which the page can't do alone.
  • Google AI Overviews, not just ChatGPT — KB: AI Overviews is the fastest surface and was 57% of Toku's AI mentions. We've only tested ChatGPT. Submit the page in Search Console and check it surfaces in AIO + Perplexity.
  • Don't let it become fragment #5 — link the agency listicles + comparison pages to this entity hub so citations concentrate, per the "concentrate don't narrow" KB row.
