Two glaring gaps

97% of our articles have fewer than 2 conversion CTAs. The article gets cited by an AI, the reader lands on the page, and there's nowhere obvious to go next. Citations without CTAs = traffic without pipeline. This is the single most pervasive structural issue and the easiest to fix in bulk.

79% of our articles where it applies are missing a "What is X?" definition section. This is the AEO category-vocabulary play — without it, AI models can't anchor the article to a category and substitute someone else's definition (like Magna's).
