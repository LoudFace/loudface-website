Motivation

When a buyer asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews about B2B software, the answer links to sources. Which sources those are decides whose facts the buyer reads. We pooled five live projects to count them: 160,240 citations, every link an AI engine surfaced inside an answer.

The short version: company websites won half of everything AI cited, but one community forum beat every single company domain. Below is what we measured, every table behind it, and what to change about your own AEO because of it.

Results

Company websites win half of everything

Corporate content is the most-cited source type by a wide margin. Across all three engines, company sites took 50.7% of citations (Figure 1). The next type, user-generated content, sat at 13.2%. Nothing else cleared 12%.

This held in every single project. Corporate ranged from 41.6% to 66.9% across the five brands, and it was the top type in all of them. If an AI answer cites a single source about your category, the odds favour a company website saying it.

Reddit is the single most-cited domain, and it is a ChatGPT habit

Source type is one lens. Individual domains tell a sharper story. Corporate wins as a category because thousands of company sites add up; no single corporate domain dominates. Reddit does, with 7.0% of all citations on its own.

Look at the top-engine column and the pattern jumps out: 80.5% of Reddit’s citations came from ChatGPT. Same forum, completely different treatment depending on which engine answers.

The three engines lean on different sources

If you optimize for “AI search” as one thing, you are averaging across machines that disagree. Corporate comes first on every engine, but the edges differ: ChatGPT leans hardest on user-generated content, Perplexity is the most editorial, and AI Overviews pulls the least reference content.

Owned pages get cited hardest when they get pulled

Citation share tells you how often a source shows up. It does not tell you how efficiently a page turns attention into a citation. For that we use citation rate: when an engine pulls a page into its working set, how reliably does it cite it. Owned pages lead at 2.04, ahead of competitor pages (0.95) and UGC (0.90).

The practical read: chasing retrieval (getting crawled, getting pulled) is a different job from chasing citation (getting quoted). You want both, and the two levers are not the same page edits.

Limitations

  • Three engines only.ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews returned data this window. We make no claim about Claude, Copilot or Grok.
  • Pooled totals are weighted by project size.Two projects contributed close to 6,800 conversations each, the smallest 1,665. Every finding was also checked per project.
  • One 30-day snapshot.Engine behaviour shifts. A number true in May is a hypothesis in August until you measure again.
