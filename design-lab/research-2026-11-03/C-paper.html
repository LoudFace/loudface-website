Executive summary

Company websites win the category. A forum wins the leaderboard.

Across 160,240 citations from five B2B SaaS brands, company websites won half of everything AI cited. One domain still beat them all: Reddit, cited 11,237 times, more than twice the next domain.

  • 50.7%Company websites took half of all citations, first in each of the five projects.
  • 80.5%of Reddit's citations came through ChatGPT. No domain was cited more.
  • 15.0%of ChatGPT's citations went to user content, the most of any engine. Perplexity leans editorial.
  • 2.04Owned pages are cited 2.04 times per retrieval, the highest of any type.
  • 8 of 8source types were cited more than retrieved: two jobs, not one.
Arnel BukvaFounder and Head of Growth
5 findings · 6 figures

Finding 1 · Source types

50.7%

Company websites took half of every citation, and came first in each of the five projects.

81,218 of 160,240 citations. Nothing else cleared 14%.

Finding 2 · Domains

80.5%

Reddit is the single most-cited domain, and 80.5% of its citations came through ChatGPT.

Same forum, very different treatment depending on which engine answers.

Finding 3 · Engines

15.0%

ChatGPT leans hardest on user content, Perplexity on editorial, and AI Overviews barely uses reference sites.

Corporate came first on every engine in every project. The edges differ.

Finding 4 · Citation rate

2.04

When an engine pulls an owned page, it cites it 2.04 times per retrieval, more than any other source type.

Toku ranks first of 1,425 cited domains in its category; loudface.co second of 2,160.

Finding 5 · Retrieval

8of 8

Every source type was cited more than it was retrieved: getting pulled and getting quoted are two different jobs.

A page can be retrieved all day and cited rarely. The two levers are not the same page edits.
