Key takeaways

  • Get into the Reddit conversation if you care about ChatGPT. ChatGPT drives 80.5% of Reddit’s citations. Make sure your category’s real threads are accurate and current.
  • Structure owned pages to be quoted, not just crawled. Owned pages are cited 2.04 times per retrieval, but only when the engine can lift a clean, self-contained answer.
  • Optimise per engine, not for “AI” as one thing. Perplexity is the most editorial engine, ChatGPT the most community-heavy, AI Overviews the lightest on reference sources.

Motivation

When a buyer asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews about B2B software, the answer links to sources. Which sources those are decides whose facts the buyer reads. We pooled five live projects to count them: 160,240 citations, every link an AI engine surfaced inside an answer, from 23,545 conversations in the 30 days to 1 June 2026.

Company websites win half of everything

Corporate content is the most-cited source type by a wide margin. Across all three engines, company sites took 50.7% of citations (Figure 1). The next type, user-generated content, sat at 13.2%. Nothing else cleared 12%.

This held in every project. Corporate ranged from 41.6% to 66.9% across the five brands, and it was the top type in all of them.

Reddit is the single most-cited domain

Source type is one lens; individual domains tell a sharper story. Corporate wins as a category because thousands of company sites add up, and no single corporate domain dominates. Reddit does, with 7.0% of all citations on its own.

Figure 2

The nine most‑cited domains

Share of 160,240 citations, and the engine that cited each one most.

80.5% of reddit.com’s 11,237 citations came from ChatGPT. “Owned” marks a project’s own domain: two of the five brands, Toku and LoudFace, appear in the top nine on their own sites.

Read the right-hand column and the pattern is plain: the same forum gets completely different treatment depending on which engine answers.

The three engines lean on different sources

If you optimise for “AI search” as one thing, you are averaging across machines that disagree. Corporate comes first on every engine, but the edges differ: ChatGPT leans hardest on user-generated content (15.0%), Perplexity is the most editorial (13.6%), and AI Overviews pulls the least reference content (3.5%).

Figure 3

Source mix by engine

Share of each engine’s citations, %. The six largest source types; Other and Institutional (2.0% each overall) are left out.

Owned pages get cited hardest when they get pulled

Citation share tells you how often a source shows up. It does not tell you how efficiently a page turns attention into a citation. For that we use citation rate: when an engine pulls a page into its working set, how reliably it cites it. Owned pages lead at 2.04, ahead of competitor pages (0.95) and user-generated content (0.90).

Figure 4

Citation rate against retrieval rate

By source type. Every type was cited more than it was retrieved.

Owned pages show the widest gap: 2.04 against 0.78. A page can be retrieved all day and cited rarely, so getting pulled and getting quoted are separate jobs.

Limitations

  • Three engines only.ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews returned data this window. We make no claim about Claude, Copilot or Grok.
  • Pooled totals are weighted by project size.Two projects contributed close to 6,800 conversations each, the smallest 1,665. Every finding was also checked per project.
  • One 30-day snapshot.Engine behaviour shifts. A number true in May is a hypothesis in August until it is measured again.
