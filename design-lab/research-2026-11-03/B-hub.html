What AI actually cites: the B2B SaaS AI-Citation Benchmark (2026)

Across 160,240 citations from five B2B SaaS brands, company websites took 50.7%. Reddit was the single most-cited domain, almost entirely through ChatGPT. Owned pages were cited 2.04 times per retrieval, more than any other source type.
