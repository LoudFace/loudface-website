What AI actually cites: the B2B SaaS AI-Citation Benchmark (2026)

Abstract

We pooled 30 days of AI-citation data from five live B2B SaaS projects: 23,545 sampled conversations in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, and the 160,240 citations inside them. Company websites took 50.7% of all citations and were the top source type in every project. Reddit was the single most-cited domain, at 7.0%, almost entirely through ChatGPT. Owned pages were cited 2.04 times per retrieval, more than any other type.

Short answer

Across 160,240 AI citations from five B2B SaaS brands, company websites won half of everything AI cited (50.7%), but Reddit was the single most-cited source, more than twice the next domain. Corporate content wins the category; a community forum wins the leaderboard, almost entirely through ChatGPT.

How we measured this

We pooled AI-citation data from five live B2B SaaS projects we run inside Peec AI: LoudFace, Toku, and three anonymized clients. The window was 30 days ending 1 June 2026. The instrument logged 23,545 sampled conversations and 160,240 individual citations, which is every link an AI engine surfaced inside an answer.

Three engines returned data this window: ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. Claude, Copilot and Grok were not measured, and nothing here describes them.

Source types come from Peec's own domain classification, which buckets every cited domain into one of eight categories. Larger projects pull more weight in the pooled totals, so we checked each finding per project to confirm it is not one big client talking.

“You” is a project's own domain. “Competitor” is a domain Peec tagged as a rival for that project. Null classifications were folded into “Other”, 2.0% of citations.

Company websites win half of everything

Corporate content is the most-cited source type by a wide margin. Across all three engines, company sites took 50.7% of citations (Figure 1). The next type, user-generated content, sat at 13.2%. Nothing else cleared 12%. If an AI answer cites a single source about your category, the odds favour a company website saying it.

This held in every project: Corporate ranged from 41.6% to 66.9% across the five brands and was the top type in all of them.

Reddit is the most-cited domain, and it is a ChatGPT habit

Corporate wins as a category because thousands of company sites add up. No single corporate domain dominates. Reddit does: it took 7.0% of all citations on its own, more than twice the second domain, toku.com, at 3.6%.

Look at the top-engine column. Of Reddit's 11,237 citations, 9,050 came from ChatGPT, which cited the forum at 2.64 citations per retrieval. Perplexity barely touched it: 335 citations against 1,252 retrievals. Same forum, a different treatment depending on which engine answers.

toku.com and loudface.co are projects' own domains. Toku ranks first of 1,425 cited domains in its category; LoudFace ranks second of 2,160.

The three engines have different personalities

If you optimize for “AI search” as one thing, you are averaging across machines that disagree. Each engine leans on a different source mix, and the edges reward different moves.

Owned pages get cited hardest when they get pulled

Citation share tells you how often a source shows up. It does not tell you how efficiently a page turns attention into a citation. For that we use citation rate: when an engine pulls a page into its working set, how reliably does it cite it? Owned pages lead at 2.04, far ahead of competitor pages (0.95) and UGC (0.90). Corporate at large sits at 0.72, because the corporate bucket includes many pages that get crawled and ignored.

Three of the five brands are a top-two cited source in their own category.

Every line rises: every source type was cited more often than it was retrieved, so citation and retrieval are not the same signal.

Being cited is not the same as being retrieved

UGC is retrieved more than any other third-party type (0.413), but its citation rate (0.90) sits well below owned pages, so a lot of what gets retrieved never makes the answer. Owned pages flip that: retrieved at 0.775 and cited at 2.04. Engines reach for them less often, but when they do, the page earns the link. Chasing retrieval and chasing citation are different jobs, and the two levers are not the same page edits.

What this means for your AEO

Three moves come straight out of the data.

  • Get into the Reddit conversation if you care about ChatGPT. ChatGPT drives 80.5% of Reddit's citations. Make sure your category's real threads are accurate, current, and mention you where it is honest to.
  • Structure owned pages to be quoted, not just crawled. Owned pages cite at 2.04 per retrieval, but only if the engine can lift a clean, self-contained answer: short definitional blocks, direct claims, clear headers.
  • Optimize per engine instead of for “AI” as one blob. Perplexity wants editorial sourcing (13.6%), ChatGPT community and reference signals, Google AI Overviews authoritative corporate pages (53.4%).

Method

Limitations

This is a three-engine benchmark. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews are all that returned data this window; it makes no claim about Claude, Copilot or Grok.

The pooled totals are weighted by project size. Toku and one other anonymized client each contributed close to 6,800 conversations, while the smallest project contributed 1,665. Every finding was checked per project, and the directional patterns held in each.

This is a single 30-day snapshot. Engine behaviour shifts.

Patterns that held in every project: Corporate first, owned pages cite hardest, ChatGPT's Reddit habit.
