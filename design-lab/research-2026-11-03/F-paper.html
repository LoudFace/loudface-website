Why we measured this

Which sources do AI engines actually put in their answers?

Buyers of B2B software now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews which tool to choose, and each answer carries links. Those links decide which pages get read. We pulled every link the three engines surfaced for five B2B SaaS brands over 30 days to 1 June 2026: 160,240 citations across 23,545 conversations.

Each cited domain was classified into one of eight source types: corporate sites, user content such as forums and video, editorial, reference, competitors, the brand’s own site, institutional sources and other.

Finding 1 · Source types

Company websites take half of everything AI cites

Corporate sites earned 50.7% of all citations, and came first in each of the five projects. The next type, user content, took 13.2%; editorial 11.1%; reference 8.0%; competitors 7.9%. The brand’s own site took 5.0%.

The pattern held on every engine, so it is not one engine’s habit. It is what all three reach for when a buyer asks about software: the pages companies publish about their own products and categories.

Finding 2 · Domains

One forum is the most-cited domain, through one engine

Company websites win as a type, but no single company site comes close to Reddit. reddit.com was cited 11,237 times, 7.0% of every citation in the benchmark, and 80.5% of those came through ChatGPT.

The rest of the top nine is a mix of competitors in the sampled categories (riseworks.io, remote.com, deel.com), video (youtube.com, 72.4% of it through AI Overviews) and editorial (investopedia.com and businessinsider.com). Two brands in the sample, toku.com and loudface.co, reach the top nine on their own domains.

Finding 3 · Engines

The engines agree on the leader and differ at the edges

Corporate sites lead on every engine. Below them, each engine has its own lean. ChatGPT gives user content 15.0% of its citations, the most of the three, which is where Reddit’s lead comes from. Perplexity gives editorial 13.6%, its largest share after corporate. AI Overviews gives reference sites only 3.5%, a third of what the other two give them.

A brand’s own site holds a similar share on all three, between 4.6% and 5.4%. Its own-domain share barely moves between engines; the forum, press and reference shares do.

Finding 4 · Citation rate

An owned page that gets pulled tends to get quoted

Owned pages are a small share of all citations, 5.0%, but when an engine retrieves one it cites it 2.04 times per retrieval, more than twice the rate of any other type. Competitor pages (0.95) and user content (0.90) come next; reference sites are lowest of the named types at 0.68.

Getting retrieved and getting cited are two separate results. A page can be pulled often and quoted rarely. For a brand’s own site the second step is already strong; the work is in being retrieved for more of the questions buyers ask.

What this means for a B2B SaaS brand

Half of what the engines cite is company websites, and a brand’s own pages are quoted more often than anything else once retrieved. The exception is ChatGPT, where a forum outranks every company website, so a brand that tracks only its own domain sees only part of what ChatGPT cites in its category.

Limitations

  • Three engines only.We have no measurement of Claude, Copilot or Grok, so we make no claim about them.
  • Weighted by project size.Bigger projects move the totals more, so each finding was checked per project.
  • One 30-day snapshot.A number true in May is a hypothesis in August until it is measured again.
