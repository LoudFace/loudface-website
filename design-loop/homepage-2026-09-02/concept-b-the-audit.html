What the audit finds. What we fix.

Three gaps show up in almost every B2B SaaS site we look at. Each one maps to a track of the program.

Finding one

You are not in the answer.

Your buyers ask ChatGPT and Perplexity who to shortlist. The answer names three companies, and yours is not one of them, because nothing on your site is written in a way an engine can quote.

The fixGEO + AEO. Prompt research, citable pages and schema, entity work, and a weekly citation report across five engines.

Finding two

You rank, but for the wrong queries.

Traffic looks fine in the dashboard. Look closer and most of it is definitions and how-tos read by people who will never buy. The queries with buying intent go to someone else.

The fixSEO + content. Technical fixes, a cluster plan built around buying intent, and a publishing engine that ships every week.

Finding three

Visitors land, nobody converts.

The right people arrive and leave. The page says what the product is, not what changes for them, and the one action you want is buried under a second headline.

The fixCRO + delivery. Conversion audits and experiments, copy and UX on the pages that matter, and Webflow or another stack when the site itself is the constraint.

Who do you work with?

B2B SaaS companies, mostly Series A and later, with a product in market and a website that is not pulling its weight. We have worked across fintech, healthtech, devtools, HR tech and cybersecurity.

What does the audit actually check?

Where your company appears, or does not, across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI, Claude and Grok for the prompts buyers in your category type. You get the report by email. No sales call required.

Can we start with one service?

Yes. Start with GEO, SEO, AEO, content, conversion or a defined implementation project. We recommend the first scope after the audit and add work only when it supports the result.

We already have a site. Do we need to rebuild?

Usually not. We run the program on a stack we can work with. If the site itself is the bottleneck, we tell you and scope only the work that removes it. Most builds go from kickoff to launch in four to six weeks.

What does a month look like?

A dedicated team, weekly async updates and a monthly performance review. We track organic traffic, citations, conversion rates and qualified leads. Minimum engagement is three months.
